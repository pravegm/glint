## 3. Video storyboards

### 3A. Social teaser: "Your Duo is a mirror" (15 s, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, sound on)

**Shot:** the founder's real iPhone Duo standing open like a small laptop on a table in **real morning light**, filmed **from just behind and above**, so the hands, the hinge and both screens are all in frame. Keep every move slow and smooth, inside the play range (never flat, never slammed). Phone sound **on** (clip mic near the speaker). Real hands are fine here (not an App Store asset).

| Time | Picture | On-screen text (big, top third, clear of the phone) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Don'ts

- No "kill", "burn", "zap", "destroy" or "Fold to …": the light pops shadows, it doesn't hurt anything.
- No competitor names (Porta Nubi, Lightfold, Epinaelo, Gleam), not even as "like X but".
- No "break your hinge", "as fast as you can" or wear jokes. Slow, smooth moves on camera; never flat, never slammed.
- No "share to unlock", follow-for-codes giveaways or bought engagement.
- "iPhone Duo" only in referential use ("for iPhone Duo"), never as part of our product's name; no Apple logos.
- No price inside videos (keep it in the post text).
