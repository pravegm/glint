Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version: Glint collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What Glint collects

Nothing. Glint has no sign-up and no login. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from anyone, and it doesn't ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

The hinge on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo, Glint reads how far the phone is open, only to aim the light while you play. That reading is used there and then. It is never saved and it never leaves your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your stars, your best scores, your Today's Dawn best, your achievements and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. We can't see them. If you delete the app, they're deleted too.

Game Center (optional)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center profile.

Crash reports

If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Tracking

Glint doesn't track you across apps or websites, and it never asks for tracking permission.

Children

Glint is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

These pages have no cookies, analytics, trackers, embedded media, external fonts or scripts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you contact us

Support happens through GitHub issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. GitHub handles your GitHub account under its own privacy statement. We use what you write only to reply and fix the problem, and we'll delete or hide an issue if you ask.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and its date before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Glint, an independent game by pravegm. Privacy questions: open an issue on GitHub.
