Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. A real person reads every one, and we aim to reply within two working days. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. You can also browse existing issues to see if someone has asked already.

Playing

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

Hold the Duo however you like: standing like a laptop, or sideways like a book. One half of the screen is the mirror, the other is the garden, and the fold is the crease where the shadows come from. Every sunrise starts with the phone shut, so if it's open, Glint asks you to close it first. Then open it slowly: light pools in the crease, and from about a right angle the band of light lifts out and moves across the garden. The wider you open, the further it reaches; close it a little and it comes back. That's the whole control.

How do I play on other iPhones?

Drag anywhere on the screen, up or down, like a trackpad: your finger doesn't have to be on the light. A gold grip on a brass rail at the garden's edge follows the light. A new level starts with the light hidden in the crease and says "Drag down to let the light in". Everything in the game, including every achievement, works this way.

What's the trick?

Shadows never stand still, so light left in one place only gives each one a short dose. The light does nothing right at the crease, so meet shadows as they surface. Small ones pop as the light passes; big ones (brutes) need you to follow them, opening the phone at the pace they creep. Pops in quick succession build a chain, up to ×4, and three or more in one sweep is a Sweep, with a bonus. Deal with whatever is closest to your flowers first.

What do the fireflies do?

They drift out of the crease like the shadows, but they're friends. A quick pass of light is fine; hold the light on one too long and it fizzles, and your chain breaks. A firefly that reaches the flowers replants a flower you've lost, or blooms for bonus points if none are missing.

How do flowers and stars work?

The five flowers along the far edge are your lives. A shadow that reaches the edge eats the flower in its path. Lose all five and the night wins: try again. If you're still standing at sunrise, every shadow left melts away and you get stars: five flowers for three stars (from level 4, three stars also need the level's score), three or four for two, one or two for one. Stars open the next levels and, at two-thirds of a world's stars, the next world.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Play lives between about a right angle and well open, the range where the Duo's hinge is made to hold its position, and it rewards small, smooth movements. Opening beyond that changes nothing, so you never need to lay the phone flat, and nothing in the game rewards moving it fast. The only full close is at the start of a level, or when you want to pause.

How do I pause?

Shut the phone (on a Duo), or tap the pause button. On a Duo the outer screen shows "Paused" and your score. Open the phone again and the level carries on after a short moment, with nothing lost. Nothing ever pauses or warns you just for holding the phone around a right angle.

Can I swap which half is the mirror?

Yes, in book pose (held sideways): Settings > Swap halves. It's handy if you prefer the garden on the other side, and if your Duo briefly blurs one half of the screen when you start folding from fully flat (an iOS effect that no app can switch off), you can keep the garden on the half that stays sharp.

How do I open Long Night and Today's Dawn?

Today's Dawn opens once you've finished level 3. Long Night opens at level 5, or a day after you first play, whichever comes first. Both are on Home.

How does Today's Dawn work?

One sunrise a day, about 90 seconds, the same for everyone. Play it as often as you like; your best score of the day counts, and it goes to the Today's Dawn leaderboard if you use Game Center. A new one starts at midnight UTC (midnight UK time in winter).

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Glint plays sound even with the silent switch on. Settings has separate switches for Sound (the sizzle, pops and chimes), Music (the dawn pad) and Haptics.

Reduce motion

Settings > Reduce motion turns off the light's shimmer and any screen shake. The light, the shadows and the fireflies still move: that's the game.

The light feels laggy or jumpy on my Duo

Please tell us (an issue with a screen recording is perfect): the light is meant to follow the phone exactly and smoothly. Say whether it happens when opening slowly, when moving quickly, or when holding still.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your Long Night score, your Today's Dawn score and your total stars go to their leaderboards, and achievements are reported as you earn them. Glint works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Glint is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were playing with the hinge or by dragging, and in which pose (laptop or book);
  • what you were playing (a level number, Long Night or Today's Dawn);
  • what happened, and what you expected (for the light, roughly how far open the phone was);
  • a screen recording, if you can. It helps a lot.
