Catch the sun. Pop the shadows.

The top half of your iPhone Duo is a mirror. Open the phone and it catches the sunrise, throwing a band of warm light across the garden below. The wider you open it, the further the light reaches. Shadows creep out of the crease towards your flowers: light them and they pop. Spare the fireflies.

How it plays

Open the phone

Every sunrise starts with the phone shut, and shut means dark. Open it and light pools in the crease, then lifts out across the garden.

Aim with the angle

The wider you open, the further the light reaches. Close it a little and the light comes back. It follows the phone smoothly, both ways.

Pop the shadows

Hold the light on a shadow and it sizzles, shrinks and pops in gold sparks and petals. Sweep through a crowd and the pops climb a rising scale.

On iPhone Duo, and on every other iPhone

On iPhone Duo

Hold it however you like, as a laptop or as a book: one half is the mirror, the other is the garden. Play lives between about a right angle and well open, the range the hinge is made to hold, so you never lay the phone flat and there's nothing to gain by rushing. Shut the phone mid-level and the game pauses; the outer screen shows your score. In book pose, Settings can swap the halves.

On every other iPhone

Drag anywhere to aim the light, like a trackpad, and a gold grip on a brass rail at the garden's edge follows it. Same garden, same shadows, same scores and the same 30 sunrises. The fold is the most magical way to play, but nobody is left out. Needs iOS 18 or later.

Spare the fireflies

Fireflies drift out of the crease too. A quick pass of light is fine, but hold it on one too long and it fizzles, and your chain breaks. Let it reach your flowers and it replants a lost one. Sometimes the best move is to look away.

Five kinds of shadow

They're not bad, just gloomy. Each one wants a different move.

MurkCreeps steadily towards your flowers. The basic shadow.
WispSmall and quick, in flocks. One good sweep pops the lot.
BruteBig, slow and grumpy. Light left in one place won't finish it: follow it.
SplitterTwin-lobed. Pop it and two wisps fling out further, fast.
HopperCrouches, then jumps. The first touch of light makes it hop at once.

30 sunrises, 3 worlds

Every level is one sunrise, from 35 seconds to a minute and a half, with shadows from the first second and the sun climbing in the mirror as the clock runs. At sunrise every shadow left melts away. Keep all five flowers, and from level 4 beat the level's score too, for three stars; stars open the next world.

MeadowMurks, wisps and your first brutes. Fireflies arrive.
RooftopsSplitters and hoppers join mixed flocks.
HarbourEverything, faster and denser, with fewer fireflies. Properly hard.

Ways to play

  • Sunrises: 30 handmade levels with stars to earn. Level 1 teaches as you play, one line at a time; there's no tutorial to sit through.
  • Long Night: endless waves that never let up, and one best score to beat.
  • Today's Dawn: one sunrise a day, the same for everyone. Your best of the day counts.

Pay once. That's it.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and Glint collects no data. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Every sound, from the sizzle to the rising pops and the birdsong at sunrise, is made live in the app. Game Center is optional.
